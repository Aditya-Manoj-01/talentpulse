# People Analytics Privacy Policy

## Aggregate-only reporting
People analytics results are reported only for groups of at least 5 employees. Any cell, filter or breakdown that would describe fewer than 5 employees is suppressed.

## No individual predictions
Individual-level attrition risk scores are never shown to managers. Attrition models may only be used to inform cohort-level interventions such as workload, pay or policy changes.

## Prohibited uses
Attrition risk scores must not be used in performance ratings, promotion decisions, compensation decisions or terminations.

## Fairness audits
Attrition models are audited for fairness across gender and age groups every quarter. If the equalized odds difference exceeds 0.10 for any group, the model must be reviewed before further use.

## Data minimisation and PII
Names, email addresses, phone numbers and employee IDs are masked before any analytics query is processed. Analysts work with pseudonymised data only.

## Retention
Employee analytics data is retained for 24 months after an employee exits and is then deleted.

## Access and contact
Access to people analytics tools is role-based and reviewed every 6 months. Privacy questions go to the Data Protection Officer at privacy@northwind.example.

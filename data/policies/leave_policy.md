# Leave Policy

## Annual leave
Employees receive 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, accrued monthly at 1.5 days per month. Up to 10 unused days may be carried forward into the next year; anything above 10 days lapses on 31 December.

## Applying for leave
Leave of more than 3 consecutive days must be applied for at least 7 days in advance. Shorter leave can be applied for 1 day in advance.

## Sick leave
Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for sick leave longer than 2 consecutive days. Sick leave cannot be carried forward.

## Parental leave
Birthing parents receive 26 weeks of paid maternity leave. Non-birthing parents receive 10 working days of paid paternity leave, to be taken within 6 months of the child's birth or adoption.

## Bereavement leave
Employees receive 5 days of paid bereavement leave on the death of an immediate family member.

## Leave encashment
On exit, unused annual leave is encashed at the base salary rate up to a maximum of 30 days.

# Overtime Policy

Applies to all full-time employees of Northwind Analytics Pvt. Ltd. (a fictional company used for this demo).

## Approval
Overtime must be pre-approved by the employee's direct manager in the HRMS portal before the extra hours are worked. Retroactive approval is allowed only for production incidents and must be filed within 2 working days.

## Limits
Overtime is capped at 12 hours per week and 50 hours per calendar quarter. No employee may work more than 3 consecutive weeks with overtime; a fourth consecutive week requires approval from the department head.

## Compensation
Weekday overtime is paid at 1.5 times the base hourly rate. Overtime on weekends and public holidays is paid at 2 times the base hourly rate. Employees at job level 4 and above are not paid for overtime but receive compensatory time off instead.

## Compensatory time off
Compensatory time off (comp-off) is credited hour-for-hour and must be used within 30 days of being earned, otherwise it lapses.

## Wellbeing review
When an employee logs overtime in 3 consecutive weeks, HR automatically schedules a wellbeing review with the employee and manager within 10 working days. The review covers workload, staffing and whether the work can be redistributed.

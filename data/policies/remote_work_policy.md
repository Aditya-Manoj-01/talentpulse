# Hybrid and Remote Work Policy

## Hybrid model
The default working model is hybrid, with employees expected in the office 3 days per week. Teams choose their anchor days together with their manager.

## Eligibility
Employees become eligible for hybrid work after completing the 90-day probation period. Roles that require on-site equipment, such as laboratory roles, may be exempt from hybrid eligibility.

## Core hours
Core collaboration hours are 11:00 to 16:00 IST, during which employees are expected to be reachable regardless of location.

## Stipend
Eligible hybrid employees receive an internet and home-office stipend of INR 2,000 per month, paid with salary.

## Working from abroad
Working remotely from outside India is limited to 14 calendar days per year and requires prior approval from both the manager and the HR business partner because of tax and data-protection obligations.

## Requests
Requests for a fully remote arrangement are made through the manager, reviewed by the HR business partner, and re-evaluated every 6 months.

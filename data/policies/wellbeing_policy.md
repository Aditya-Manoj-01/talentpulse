# Employee Wellbeing Policy

## Employee Assistance Programme
All employees and their dependants can access the Employee Assistance Programme (EAP), which includes 6 free, confidential counselling sessions per year.

## Mental health days
Employees receive 2 paid mental health days per year in addition to sick leave. These days do not require a medical certificate.

## Meeting-free time
Friday afternoons after 14:00 are meeting-free across the company so employees can do focused work.

## Weekend work
No employee should work more than 2 consecutive weekends. Managers must rebalance the roster if this limit would be exceeded.

## Burnout check-ins
Managers hold a monthly one-to-one that includes a workload and burnout check-in. Persistent overtime is a trigger for an additional check-in.

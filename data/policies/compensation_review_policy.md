# Compensation Review Policy

## Annual cycle
Compensation is reviewed once a year, with changes effective from 1 April.

## Merit increase bands
Merit increases are based on the performance rating: rating 5 receives 12–15%, rating 4 receives 8–11%, rating 3 receives 4–7%, and ratings of 2 or below receive no merit increase.

## Promotions
A promotion to the next job level carries a minimum salary increase of 10%, in addition to any merit increase.

## Market adjustments
Employees whose compa-ratio (salary divided by the midpoint of their pay band) is below 0.85 are eligible for an off-cycle market adjustment, reviewed each October.

## Equity
Annual stock option refreshes are granted to employees at job level 4 and above who are rated 4 or 5.

## Pay equity audit
An annual pay equity audit compares pay by gender within the same job level and role. Any unexplained gap greater than 3% must be remediated in the next review cycle.

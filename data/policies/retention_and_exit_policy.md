# Retention and Exit Policy

## Stay interviews
Managers conduct stay interviews twice a year with every employee rated 4 or 5 in the last performance cycle. Stay interviews focus on growth, workload and recognition, and are separate from performance reviews.

## Retention bonus
A retention bonus may be offered to an employee who has resigned or is at risk of leaving. It requires joint approval from the HR business partner and the department head, and is capped at 20% of annual base salary. A retention bonus must be repaid in full if the employee leaves within 12 months of receiving it.

## Counter-offers
Counter-offers to resigning employees are limited to a maximum salary increase of 20% and may be extended only once per employee.

## Notice period
The notice period is 60 days for employees at job level 3 and above and 30 days for job levels 1 and 2.

## Exit interviews
Exit interviews are conducted by HR, not the line manager, within the last 5 working days of employment. Exit interview responses are reported only in aggregate, for groups of at least 5 leavers.

## Rehire
Former employees who left in good standing are eligible for rehire after 6 months.
